# Privacy Policy — Tennis Tracker

**Last updated:** April 2026

## Overview

Tennis Tracker is a sports utility app that helps you follow upcoming tennis matches and tournaments for ATP players. This policy explains how we handle your data.

## Data We Collect

**We do not collect any personal data.** The app does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, crash reporters, or any form of tracking.

## Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores your list of selected (followed) players locally on your device using App Group storage. This data:

- Never leaves your device
- Is not transmitted to any server
- Is shared only between the main app and the home screen widget on the same device
- Can be cleared by deleting the app

## Network Requests

The app fetches a publicly available JSON configuration file containing player profiles, match schedules, and tournament data. These requests:

- Do not include any user-identifiable information
- Do not use cookies or authentication
- Are made to GitHub Pages (`stvl.github.io`)

## Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or frameworks that collect user data.

## Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
